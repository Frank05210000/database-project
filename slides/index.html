Relational Schema · 關聯綱要

15 張資料表，規則寫進資料庫15 tables, with the rules enforced inside the database

PK
複合主鍵處理 M:N 與弱實體cart_item (user, product)、order_item (order, item_no)Composite keys for M:N and weak entities
CK
CHECK 限制取值範圍價格 > 0、庫存 ≥ 0、訂單狀態只能是 6 種之一、折扣率介於 0~1Value-level rules: price, stock, status, rate
FK
複合外鍵守住折扣期間product_discount 複製期間並以複合 FK 對應 discount，確保一致Composite FK keeps discount periods consistent
EX
EXCLUDE 防止期間重疊 (BR-13)同一商品的特殊活動折扣，日期區間不能重疊EXCLUDE USING gist — no overlapping periods per product

BR-03
商品與商店的關係？一筆訂單能同時買不同商店的商品嗎？Product–store relationship? Can one order span stores?
一商品一商店，訂單可跨店One store per product; orders may span stores
BR-15
折扣率怎麼表示？題目寫 0.05%，我們假設是筆誤、實際指 5%。“0.05%” looks like a typo — we assume 5%
小數 0~1，0.05 = 折 5%Decimal in (0,1): 0.05 = 5% off
BR-22
稅率和基本運費要定多少？What tax rate and base shipping fee?
稅 5%、運費 100 元（存成系統參數）5% tax, NT$100 shipping — stored as settings
BR-19 · 25
要不要讓顧客取消訂單？不同狀態申請退貨怎麼處理？Cancellation, and returns at each order status?
買後可申請退貨；received 自動取消，之後需廠家同意Auto-cancel at “received”; later needs the store’s approval
BR-10
訂單金額的計算順序與稅基？Calculation order and the tax base?
先扣折扣，再算稅Apply discounts first, then tax
BR-12
多筆折扣同時有效時怎麼套用？How do multiple active discounts combine?
季節性折扣依序連乘；運費折扣達任一門檻即免運Seasonal rates multiply; any shipping threshold waives it
